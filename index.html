How each week works

Upper and lower body twice a week each, so every muscle gets worked twice. Runs come the day after an upper-body day, never after legs, so your legs are fresh for them. One full rest day.

The rules

Make the last 2 reps hard

Pick a weight where the last couple of reps are a real effort but your form stays clean. If it's easy, it's too light.

Go up when you hit the top

When every set reaches the top of the rep range (e.g. 3 × 12), move to the next dumbbell or plate the following session.

Rest 60–90 seconds

Up to 2 minutes on squats, hip thrusts and deadlifts. 30 seconds between exercises in a superset or circuit.

Protein every day

About 1.6 g per kg of bodyweight (~85 g if you're 55 kg). The post-workout shake counts. Muscle gets built from it.

Don't crash diet

You're already lean. Eat about normal amounts; the lifting and running will change how your body looks. Sleep 7–8 hours.

Pain means stop

A burn is fine. A sharp or joint pain, or feeling dizzy, means stop that exercise and tell me.
